E-commerce customer segmentation

UCI Online Retail dataset · Nov–Dec 2025

  • SQL
  • Excel
  • Power BI
  • RFM

I cleaned 500K+ transactions in SQL and built an RFM model in Excel that sorts 4,338 customers into 5 segments. The top 22% ("Champions") bring in 65% of revenue, and the UK alone accounts for 82%.

AI drafted the SQL for RFM scoring. I checked each query's logic and output against the source data, then used Key Influencers to see which behaviors predict high-value customers.

HR employee attrition analysis

IBM HR Analytics dataset · Jan–Feb 2025

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Pivot tables

Attrition across 1,470 employees is 16.1%. Overtime is the standout driver: 30.5% of people working overtime leave, against 10.4% of those who don't. Income level and job satisfaction also predict who leaves.

I used the Decomposition Tree and Key Influencers to rank drivers, then cross-checked them against manual pivot tables. The dashboard lets HR monitor risk by department and role.
